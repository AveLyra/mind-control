# Population Influence Agenda

This agenda expands the loaded-dice directional-drift dry run into explicit
experiment families. It is a planning map, not evidence that any pathway occurs
in the real world.

## Claim Ledger

## Experiment Families

### Polarization

Question: does curated exposure create a directional vector toward threat,
anger, outgroup coldness, and institutional distrust?

Minimum controls:

- null feed or no-exposure control;
- generic political broadcast;
- wrong-direction or cross-cutting exposure;
- contemporaneous common-shock control.

### Demobilization And Nullification

Question: can curated exposure reduce political efficacy, participation intent,
or willingness to act, donate, persuade, vote, or organize?

This is the main "nullifying partisan effort" pathway. The effect of interest is
not conversion to the opposing view. It is reduced action probability among a
targeted group, especially if the opposing group is not equivalently
demobilized.

Minimum controls:

- neutral civic-information exposure;
- ordinary campaign-contact exposure;
- fatigue or doomscrolling control;
- group-specific dropout/nonresponse audit.

### Shared-Reality Fragmentation

Question: does curation reduce overlap in which facts, institutions, events, or
harms different groups treat as salient?

Minimum controls:

- common factual brief;
- same facts with different emotional framing;
- no-target and wrong-target profiles;
- delayed retest to separate immediate salience from persistence.

### Commercial Activation

Question: does affective loading change consumer impulse, urgency, risk
tolerance, or brand/product affinity?

Minimum controls:

- price and seasonality controls;
- generic ad exposure;
- wrong-person targeting;
- delayed purchase-intent or behavior follow-up.

The related low-awareness visual-processing question is documented in
`docs/vindication-blurry-ad-low-awareness-processing.md`. It should not be
treated as evidence of subliminal advertising from a retrospective anecdote.

The related device question is documented in
`docs/vindication-tooth-mounted-sensing-power.md`. It is a local measurement
pathway, not a proposed detector of thoughts, hidden messages, or targeting.

The magnetic-effects literature and its evidence boundaries are documented in
`docs/vindication-magnetic-effects-research.md`.

The expanded record adds a receiver-first rule: field exposure, receiver
operation, neural decoding, and behavioral influence are separate endpoints.
Evidence for one must not be used as evidence for the next.

### State/Timestep Amplification

Question: does delivery timing, fatigue, notification interruption, or
state-cueing amplify curated content effects?

Minimum controls:

- sham timing;
- wrong-state timing;
- notification-only or content-only controls;
- awareness and expectancy checks.

### Financial Notification Interruption

Question: do financial-stress notifications, such as balance, overdraft, bill,
market, debt, tax, or account alerts, disrupt concentration more than neutral
notifications, especially when they arrive during high-load or emotionally
vulnerable task states?

This is a candidate attention-disruption and reactivation pathway. It should be
tested separately from semantic association because the active ingredient could
be financial threat, unexpected onset, vibration/sound, task timing, or a
combination of those factors.

Minimum controls:

- neutral notifications matched for novelty and urgency;
- unrelated negative notifications matched for emotional valence;
- sham and random-time notification windows;
- silent banner versus vibration versus sound channel separation;
- baseline financial stress, income volatility, anxiety, sleep, and ordinary
  notification volume;
- task-state logging before outcomes are inspected.

### Semantic Self-Generation And Kinesthetic Pairing

Question: does getting a person to generate target-cluster language themselves
create stronger later salience than passive exposure, and can body-linked cues
such as speech production, typing, posture, movement, breathing rhythm, or
phone-like vibration amplify or reactivate the association?

This is the candidate "trigger word" and kinesthetic pathway. It should be
treated as an association mechanism, not as proof of targeting. The important
distinction is whether the participant's own action adds measurable association
strength beyond hearing or reading the same content.

Minimum controls:

- passive exposure with matched word counts;
- negative-but-unrelated word clusters;
- neutral word clusters;
- wrong-movement, wrong-posture, or wrong-vibration controls;
- awareness, suspicion, demand-characteristic, and expectancy probes;
- baseline mood, vocabulary, stress, and trait rumination measures.

### Acoustic Feature And Sleep-State Coding

Question: can measurable acoustic properties such as pitch, spectral balance,
attack, rhythm, or low-frequency energy produce state-specific detection or
reactivation after an explicit, consented pairing phase?

This is a narrower extension of state/timing amplification. It must distinguish
feature identity from ordinary loudness, startle, transducer vibration,
awakening, sleep fragmentation, expectancy, and selective memory. The dedicated
protocol is in `docs/vindication-acoustic-feature-sleep-state-coding.md`.

Minimum controls:

- equalized sound pressure, duration, rise time, and measured startle;
- sham, random-time, wrong-feature, and wrong-state cues;
- verified EEG or validated stage scoring with clock-synchronization checks;
- blinded dream and memory scoring plus awareness and expectancy probes;
- explicit pairing only, with benign stimuli and independent ethics review.

## Data Ingestion Targets

Faraday should prefer datasets that can express before-after or exposed-control
movement in the vector dimensions. Candidate classes:

- survey panels with repeated political affect, efficacy, trust, and behavior
  measures;
- randomized ad or message experiments with individual-level pre/post outcomes;
- platform-export or screen-time diaries paired with repeated survey measures;
- public political ad libraries linked to aggregate polling or turnout only as
  exploratory ecological data;
- commercial A/B tests only if condition labels, timing, and outcome definitions
  are available;
- transcripts or interaction logs with enough text to code semantic clusters,
  self-generated words, prompts, and awareness probes;
- phone or wearable logs only when haptic, posture, movement, or timing channels
  are available with consent and can be separated from semantic exposure.
- notification logs with timestamps, channel type, app category, and redacted
  semantic class when studying interruption or financial-threat salience.
- instrumented mouthguard or dental-sensor streams paired with external
  microphone, accelerometer, temperature, and reader-telemetry logs when
  studying local acoustic or mechanical exposure.

Aggregate-only data can generate hypotheses but should not be used to claim
individual-level psychological influence.

## Claim Ceiling

The strongest current claim is:

> Faraday can specify and power-test population-level response-drift hypotheses
> for polarization, demobilization, shared-reality fragmentation, commercial
> activation, timing/state amplification, semantic self-generation, and
> kinesthetic, haptic, and financial-notification interruption pathways.

Unsupported claims include:

> The tests prove real platform manipulation, direct mind control, state action,
> or the intent of any institution.
