# Optical Input And Sleep-State Influence

This note records a distinct optical pathway for the Vindication Machine. It
does not establish covert communication, subliminal advertising effects, or
targeting of any individual.

The canonical local Faraday workspace is
`workspaces/vindication-machine/optical-sleep/faraday`, inquiry
`optical-sleep-input`, currently `clarifying`. Its three proposals are
`hyp-aa19ae8b473c` (neural response), `hyp-aa1a7137cb98` (sleep-state
physiology), and `hyp-9ba759d9b04a` (non-lucid semantic transfer); all remain
`unreviewed`.

## Evidence Boundary

The literature supports several different endpoints:

- **Neural detection:** nearly imperceptible spectral flicker can produce a
  stimulus-frequency steady-state visual evoked potential (SSVEP), including a
  40 Hz response. This is a measurable neural response, not semantic decoding.
- **Sleep-state processing:** flicker delivered through closed eyelids can
  evoke SSVEPs during wakefulness and NREM sleep, with frequency-dependent
  differences across wake, light sleep, and deep sleep. Light input is
  attenuated and transformed by sleep; it is not simply equivalent to waking
  vision.
- **Physiological modulation:** light can affect melatonin, sleepiness, or
  circadian timing even when apparent color or luminance is matched. This is a
  physiological endpoint, not evidence that a sleeper understood a message.
- **Lucid REM communication:** in a multicenter study, some verified lucid
  dreamers answered simple questions and arithmetic during REM sleep using
  prespecified eye or facial signals. The successful participants were lucid
  and consciously recognized the interaction inside the dream.
- **Engineered receivers:** invisible infrared can carry information when an
  added device converts it into visible light. This is receiver-mediated
  communication, not unaided perception.

The necessary distinctions are:

`signal reaches retina or visual pathways` != `neural response is measurable`
!= `pattern is discriminated` != `pattern has learned meaning` != `language is
understood` != `information is retained or changes later behavior`.

## Unresolved Question

The important open test is whether temporally encoded optical stimulation can
transmit a prespecified semantic message to a non-lucid sleeper without the
person consciously experiencing it, with comprehension demonstrated by
preregistered behavioral or later-memory measures. Existing findings do not
establish that end-to-end chain.

## Safety And Falsification

Any future study would require verified sleep staging, retinal and circadian
safety controls, sham and no-light conditions, randomized message assignment,
post-awakening awareness checks, delayed memory tests, and a strict separation
between EEG detection and semantic comprehension. A group-average SSVEP or
melatonin change must not be treated as evidence of message reception.
