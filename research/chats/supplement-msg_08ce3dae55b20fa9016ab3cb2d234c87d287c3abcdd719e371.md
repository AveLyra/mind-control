The most useful protection is to **control exposure and timing, then slow down consequential decisions**. That addresses the plausible channels in our notes without assuming that any covert campaign is occurring. The project’s influence agenda is a set of hypotheses; its dry runs do not show that people were targeted or controlled. | Pathway we outlined | Practical defense |
| --- | --- |
| Curated feeds and targeted ads | Choose sources deliberately, use subscription or chronological views where available, and review ad and tracking settings. These steps reduce some personalized exposure; they do not eliminate persuasion. [FTC privacy guide](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2021/06/your-guide-protecting-your-privacy-online) |
| Emotional framing, repetition, and “trigger” themes | Notice the *technique*—urgency, fear, scapegoating, or a forced choice—then check the underlying claim with an independent source. Experiments suggest that learning to recognize such techniques improves misinformation detection, though protection varies by message. [Inoculation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36001675/) |
| Notifications timed around work or financial stress | Silence nonessential alerts during focused work and sleep; check financial accounts at chosen times while keeping genuinely urgent alerts. Both [iPhone Focus](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/allow-or-silence-notifications-for-a-focus-iph21d43af5b/27/ios/27) and [Android Do Not Disturb](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9069335?hl=en) support this. |
| Sleep, sound, and light cues | Keep the bedroom quiet and dark and silence optional media and alerts. This is sound sleep advice; it is **not** evidence that hidden messages are being delivered. [CDC sleep guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/), [project evidence boundary] (private local reference omitted) |

For purchases, donations, political commitments, or other important choices, set a simple rule in advance: wait until you are rested, write down your reason, and check one independent source before acting. If a particular app or alert pattern concerns you, record **actual exposures and timestamps**, including ordinary ones, before drawing a conclusion. A striking coincidence alone cannot establish targeting or intent; the notification note identifies routine schedules, base rates, and selective memory as competing explanations. The strongest objection to a more elaborate defense plan is that it could treat unproven magnetic, optical, or sleep-based control as established. Our records do not support that step. Ordinary privacy, attention, sleep, and device security measures are proportionate; specialized shielding or “counter-signals” have no demonstrated basis in these findings.
