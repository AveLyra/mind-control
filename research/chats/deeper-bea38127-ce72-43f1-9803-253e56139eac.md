**Concealment and effective conditioning are separate requirements.** Showing that someone overlooks a cue would not show that it produces a specific, lasting psychological effect.

A sound heard but interpreted as ordinary house creaking would be **camouflaged, not necessarily subliminal**. Likewise, perceiving two stimuli without recognizing their association differs from not perceiving the stimuli at all—a distinction that matters in conditioning research.  [source 0](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32980638/)

An awake bystander’s failure to notice something would not establish what the participant perceived. **Awareness must be measured in the person whose learning is being tested**, rather than inferred from another person’s report or from absent later recall. Experiments have found that different awareness measures can produce misleading conclusions about supposedly unconscious conditioning.  [source 1](https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000757)

The evidence does not support treating subliminality as an advantage that makes conditioning work. Some experiments report evaluative changes without conscious knowledge of the pairings; others find no evaluative conditioning when perceptual awareness is sufficiently low. These are different experimental conditions, not an established method for covert control.  [source 2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2020.104460)  [source 3](https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0000757)

Similarly, auditory priming experiments have demonstrated constrained changes in word-processing speed, while TMR experiments demonstrate changes involving previously learned associations. **Neither establishes reliable population control or selective manipulation through sounds disguised as building noises.** Ordinary creaks are not, by themselves, evidence of intentional signaling.  [source 4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21655277/)  [source 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4402655/)

The legitimate research question is whether a **prespecified effect persists under independently verified lack of awareness**, using consenting volunteers and harmless material. That can be investigated without developing concealment methods for nonconsensual manipulation; I won’t help engineer those methods.
