**The short answer:** yes, researchers in these areas have received government funding, including **NIH, NSF, Germany’s DFG, Switzerland’s SNSF, and, in one directly relevant case, DARPA**. I can verify the following *study teams and paper-level funding*, not an exhaustive list of every scientist in the field. A 2020 review alone covered **91 targeted-memory-reactivation experiments**. [Meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7144680/)

**Important distinction:** these are funding acknowledgments for published research, not evidence that any researcher participated in covert targeting. The results also have limits: a [double-blind acoustic-stimulation study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6831893/) changed sleep rhythms **without improving memory**, illustrating why a physiological effect should not be equated with reliable behavioral control.
