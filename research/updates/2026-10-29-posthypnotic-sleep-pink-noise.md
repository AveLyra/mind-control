## Inspection and identity boundaries

All 39 selected PMIDs supplied metadata and DOIs; 38 supplied complete provided abstracts. T38 had no PubMed abstract: a primary-publisher indexed abstract extract was inspected, while live retrieval returned 403. The retracted underlying meta-analysis is linked solely to document exclusion.

Sixteen PMC bodies were retrieved with complete contiguous coverage: T05, T06, T07, T08, T14, T16, T19, T20, T23, T24, T26, T27, T28, T30, T36 and T39. Selected methods/results/limitations and study relationships were inspected. This is not independent appraisal of every table, figure, supplement, participant record or statistical model.

Selected sections of the original T15 publication were checked in an eight-page [primary-paper PDF](https://www.uni-muenster.de/imperia/md/content/psyifp/ae_lappe/freie_dokumente/arzi2012.pdf). T17 had two connector body failures, a live PMC browser challenge, and publisher 403; subsequently recovered indexed primary results are partial inspection, not a complete body. T13 had one fresh body failure. T04's publisher returned 403 and T21's DOI route was unavailable. Earlier checks remain separately dated; these failures are not silently converted into fresh full-text success.

Publication dates below follow returned PubMed issue dates. In particular, T26 is a 2023 publication despite its later PubMed indexing, and T37 is listed under February 2026. A numeric PMID or DOI year is not used to invent a publication date.

## Evidence relationships and contrasts

Repeated cue tests, retained cue responsiveness, behavior between tests, exposure duration, and post-withdrawal persistence are different endpoints. T01–T03 share investigators and differ in design; T03 partly failed to reproduce earlier durability determinants. T05 is a methods/plans report, not another final-outcome cohort. T16 and T20 explicitly reuse earlier sleep-learning/counter-bias data. T14 reuses two hypnosis/sleep datasets. T28/T29 describe the same registered noise trial/sample and are not counted as independent trials.

Important narrowing from body inspection: T07's within-group grip increase, mixed-ANOVA effects and between-group post-score tests are distinct contrasts. T08's subjective benefits did not yield the expected physiological group effects; its later active cue was given to both groups. T30's insight comparisons were nonsignificant, despite a worse numerical pattern. T26's year-specific estimates are meta-regression predictions, not universal observed harm. No participant-level datasets, code, or scientific effect sizes were independently reanalyzed.

## Selected sources

<a id="t01"></a>
### T01 — Durability of "posthypnotic suggestions" as a function of type of suggestion and trance depth

L P Berrigan et al. (1991 Jan). [Durability of "posthypnotic suggestions" as a function of type of suggestion and trance depth](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2001895/). DOI: `10.1080/00207149108409616`. **Historical durability experiment; complete provided abstract checked.** High responders and simulators were retested six times over eight weeks, using a cue that also instructed reentry into hypnosis. Reported response declined differently by suggestion. Repeated testing/context and shared researchers with T02–T03 limit independent durability inference.

<a id="t02"></a>
### T02 — Durability of posthypnotic suggestions: type of suggestion and difficulty level

J E Trussell et al. (1996 Jul). [Durability of posthypnotic suggestions: type of suggestion and difficulty level](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8917929/). DOI: `10.1080/00029157.1996.10403363`. **Durability experiment; complete provided abstract checked.** N = 78 highly susceptible participants, retested at weeks 1, 3, 6 and 8. Passing declined; difficulty and time effects were reported. The later T03 attempt failed to reproduce the difficulty determinant.

<a id="t03"></a>
### T03 — Efficacy of induction and difficulty level in durability of post-hypnotic suggestions

J G Calandrino et al. (2001 Jul). [Efficacy of induction and difficulty level in durability of post-hypnotic suggestions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11417143/). DOI: `10.1080/00029157.2001.10403452`. **Targeted replication/extension; complete provided abstract checked.** N = 59 high responders. No advantage from the special reentry instruction; responses declined and the earlier difficulty effect failed to replicate. Shared Kurtz/Strube authorship with T02; not three independent laboratories establishing one curve.

### T04 — Behavioral persistence in carrying out a posthypnotic suggestion beyond the hypnotic context: a consideration of the role of perceived demand characteristics

<a id="t06"></a>
### T06 — The nature and persistence of the effects of posthypnotic suggestions on food preferences: The final report of an online study

Anoushiravan Zahedi et al. (2023). [The nature and persistence of the effects of posthypnotic suggestions on food preferences: The final report of an online study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37034940/). DOI: `10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1123907`. **Final registered-report outcomes; complete PMC body with selected sections inspected.** 55 recruited, 50 completed all sessions. Suggestion on/off altered preferences and simulated shopping choices at a visit 3–10 days later, without renewed induction but with renewed activation. Broad responsiveness recruitment; no consumption/weight endpoint. The methods paper is not a second outcome cohort.

<a id="t07"></a>
### T07 — Increasing handgrip strength via post-hypnotic suggestions with lasting effects

Ulrike Nieft et al. (2024 Oct 14). [Increasing handgrip strength via post-hypnotic suggestions with lasting effects](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39402088/). DOI: `10.1038/s41598-024-73117-0`. **Randomized grip comparison; complete PMC body with selected methods/results inspected.** N = 48. Later within-hypnosis cue activation improved grip by 2.9 kg, but immediate objective change and individual between-group time-point comparisons were nonsignificant. Mixed-ANOVA effects are also reported; do not interchange those contrasts or infer no treatment effect solely from a post-score test. No independent recalculation.

<a id="t08"></a>
### T08 — Post-hypnotic safety suggestion improves stress coping with long-lasting effects

Barbara Schmidt et al. (2024 Feb 12). [Post-hypnotic safety suggestion improves stress coping with long-lasting effects](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38347065/). DOI: `10.1038/s41598-024-54071-3`. **Stress/cue study; complete PMC body with selected sections inspected.** N = 60; reported stress and later negative thoughts improved, without corresponding group heart-rate/adrenaline effects. Cortisol response was inadequate. Both groups took home the active cue; later cue efficacy is not a maintained neutral-control contrast. Anxiety group-by-time change was nonsignificant.

### T09 — Post-hypnotic suggestion improves confidence and speed of memory access with long-lasting effects

Selected participants and overlapping research program with T07/T08; no participant-level identity claim.

### T15 — Humans can learn new information during sleep

Anat Arzi et al. (2012 Oct). [Humans can learn new information during sleep](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22922782/). The stringent arousal-free waking comparison had N = 6; inclusive analysis agreed. Stage-transfer analysis relaxed exclusions. Authors explicitly limit claims about awareness and later retention; no demonstrated weeks-long complex behavior.

Anat Arzi et al. (2014 Nov 12). [Olfactory aversive conditioning during sleep reduces cigarette-smoking behavior](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25392505/). Motivated quitters and short follow-up. Two PMC requests failed, live PMC challenged and publisher returned 403. Selected indexed results are not complete-body retrieval.

### T19 — Unlearning implicit social biases during sleep: A failure to replicate

Graelyn B Humiston et al. (2019). [Unlearning implicit social biases during sleep: A failure to replicate](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30682167/). Original-size effect and replication confidence intervals differed; a negative replication, not universal proof that every sleep intervention fails.
