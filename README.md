## Trace the research

- [Source catalog](sources/catalog.md): 91 recovered research records, with origins and status.
- [Coverage and recovery gaps](sources/coverage.md): what was searched, exclusions, and limits.
- [Citation corrections](sources/corrections.md): errors and unresolved citation markers.

- [Machine-readable manifest](sources/manifest.json): record IDs, paths, and SHA-256 digests.

Topic pages are editorial navigation and synthesis. `research/` preserves selected historical assistant answers and project research notes. Primary publications and original records control factual claims; a chat summary cannot independently establish them. Simulations establish only their stated computational results. Fiction and research proposals remain proposals.

## Maintain

Preserve provenance when adding research. Record negative findings and corrections alongside positive results. Keep personal medical discussions, credentials, private correspondence, and account backup URLs out of public commits. Do not silently fill missing text or turn a research objective into an achieved capability.

Run `python3 tools/verify_collection.py` to check local navigation and source-record integrity. This verifies collection structure, not scientific truth or external link availability.
