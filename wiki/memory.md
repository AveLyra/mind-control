## Separate capabilities

| Capability | Evidence or research lead | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Reconstruct semantic information from functional brain recordings | [Tang et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37127759/) | Requires trained, task-specific recording; semantic resemblance is not verbatim thought transcription. |
| Caption recalled video material | [Recovered BCI review](../research/chats/6ab14c04-9864-83ea-a250-b495d6f19c23-1-2.md) | Recognition among known candidates differs from recovering an arbitrary autobiographical episode. |
| Generate images associated with prompted imagery | [MIRAGE](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pcbi.1014263) | The recovered review describes constrained targets and substantial calibration; individual details remain uncertain. |
| Produce convincing reconstruction without recovering all its detail | [Spurious reconstruction from brain activity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40499302/) | A generator’s prior knowledge can supply plausible details that the neural signal does not establish. |
| Change later confidence or recollection | [Original imagery/source-monitoring review](../research/chats/supplement-61ab65d6-91b2-414d-a69f-599e081abc03.md) | Confidence, vividness, accuracy, and independent corroboration are different outcomes. |

These links are identifiable research sources; this collection has not replicated their decoding pipelines or independently evaluated every reported performance figure.

## What would discriminate recovery from invention?

The recovered proposal compares interviews and source material, the same workflow with visual editing, and the same workflow with neural information. Shuffled-signal controls, held-out documented scenes, separate recording sessions, and counts of unsupported additions test whether neural input contributes information. Subjective resemblance belongs alongside accuracy, not in place of it.

Preserve what a participant reported before seeing a generated reconstruction. Later agreement can reflect recognition, suggestion, ordinary revision, or some combination. It is not independent confirmation of every generated detail.

## Awareness needs its own measurement

The recovered [awareness-transition proposal](../research/chats/deeper-d3e91e42-862c-48b0-9142-8237714a614d.md) treats hypothesis disclosure as an experimental intervention. Knowing the hypothesis, believing it, perceiving the stimulus, recognizing a cue pairing, and remembering afterward are separate variables. Awareness questions can themselves reveal the hypothesis; excluding people who guessed can introduce post-treatment bias.

This is a proposed analysis framework, not a validated detector of consciousness. Its strongest test is a randomized comparison that can separate actual stimulation, disclosure, expectancy, and later reports.

The focused [awareness and unconscious visual-processing page](awareness.md) now integrates the supplied continuation review. It distinguishes awareness of flicker, patterns, and meaning, and compares subjective reports with objective access. Successful meaning choices already demonstrate objective content access; the further question can concern subjective experience or a justified direct/indirect-task comparison. The [intake record](../sources/awareness-update-intake.md) preserves source and verification limits.
