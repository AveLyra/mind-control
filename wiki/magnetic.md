# Magnetism and neural interfaces

[Home](../README.md) · [Optical signaling](optical.md) · [Evidence ledger](evidence-ledger.md) · [Research network](research-horizon.md)

The ChatGPT **Magnetoreception Applications Research** discussion and [Faraday magnetic note](../research/notes/vindication-magnetic-effects-research.md) use a receiver-first distinction: identify what receives a field, how it transduces it, and what outcome was measured.

| Mechanism | Evidence question | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Natural human magnetoreception | Reproducible physiological or behavioral response to weak fields? | An EEG response alone does not demonstrate conscious perception or message reception. |
| TMS | Nearby changing fields induce neural electrical effects | This mechanism cannot be substituted for ordinary weak-field exposure. |
| Magnetoelectric implant | Engineered receiver converts transmitted energy into electrical stimulation | Requires the receiver and its operating geometry. |
| Delivered nanoparticles / engineered proteins | Biological transduction after an intervention | Animal or cellular demonstrations do not establish an uninstrumented human channel. |
| Neural decoding | Information extracted from recordings with a trained decoder | Instrumented decoding is not distant thought interception. |

The primary [Woods et al. cortical-stimulator study](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn0858) reports an engineered battery-free stimulation device and acute human demonstration. The archive also discusses animal chronic operation; these should not become claims of established long-term human treatment.

The research retains negative weak-field studies and replication disputes alongside positive reports. Natural magnetosensitivity, externally powered implants, and recorded inner-speech decoding are distinct technologies with distinct evidence. Read/write asymmetry matters: decoding a representation does not establish that stimulation can produce the same thought.

A useful natural-sense claim would survive blinded artifact controls and independent replication, then demonstrate held-out discrimination with quantified error rates. A claim of selected behavioral influence would additionally require that behavioral outcome.
