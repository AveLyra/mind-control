The ChatGPT **Invisible Wavelengths Feasibility** discussion and the [Faraday optical note](../research/notes/vindication-optical-sleep-influence-research.md) distinguish optical wavelength from temporal modulation, and a measurable response from an understood message.

## Different pathways

| Pathway | Research endpoint | Missing inference |
|---|---|---|
| Imperceptible rapid flicker | Frequency-specific neural response in a waking interface | A computer detecting attention is not a sleeper decoding language. |
| Nearly invisible spectral flicker | Different visible spectra with little apparent change; SSVEP measurement | Matched appearance with open eyes need not survive eyelid filtering. |
| Closed-eyelid light | Sleep-state-dependent neural or circadian responses | Response amplitude does not measure comprehension. |
| Infrared upconversion lenses | Added receiver converts infrared to perceptible visible light | Engineered reception is not unaided subliminal infrared vision. |
| Specialized two-photon vision | Perception under specialized optical conditions | Not an established ordinary sleep communication channel. |
| Photobiomodulation | Physiological effects under a separate intervention mechanism | No inference to writing specific thoughts or instructions. |

The open question retained in the archived note is whether a prespecified semantic message can be received by a non-lucid sleeper without conscious experience, and later demonstrated through objective comprehension or retention. The collected sources establish pieces of that chain, not its completion.

The original note's lucid-REM citation points to commentary; use [Konkoly et al., primary study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.01.026) for the experiment. Its trained response channel and lucid experiences cannot establish unconscious optical communication. See the correction record before reusing quantitative claims.
