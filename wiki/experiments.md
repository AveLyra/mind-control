## Sleep-EDF scheduling dry run

The [results note](../research/notes/vindication-sleep-dry-run-results.md) reports a September 11, 2026 scheduling run across 153 Sleep-EDF cassette hypnograms, using seed 20260911. It reports 2,572 synthetic slow-wave markers, 9,169 N2 sham markers, and 7,330 REM off-target markers.

Those counts are historical computational results, not newly reproduced here. No sound was delivered. Outcome values were synthetic, including condition differences deliberately inserted into the generator. They do not estimate a memory, mood, sleep, or influence effect.

Under the reported standard buffer/spacing rules, 39 of 153 recordings had no eligible N3 window. That supports a narrow design warning about cue opportunities under that algorithm and dataset. It does not establish a biological treatment effect. Original result digests and artifact paths are retained in the note; the full dataset and runnable Faraday environment are not copied into this wiki.

## Synthetic interaction scaffold

The [interaction results](../research/notes/vindication-interaction-dry-run-results.md) exercise detection of an intentionally generated state-by-association interaction. Detecting an inserted effect validates only that bounded simulation path, not its existence in people.

A later [chat recheck](../research/chats/01a08679-d665-7a92-9c20-ce1843c10a68-52-28.md) reports a more serious qualification: the generator coupled cue awareness to condition, producing an apparent interaction even when the configured interaction was zero. The reported null-grid flag rates were 8.75%–20%. These are historical audit reports, not rerun results. They supersede any implication that the earlier harness's detection rates were ready for human-study power planning. The archive preserves both accounts so that the correction is visible.

## Acoustic reference campaign

The [campaign README](../research/notes/sleep-acoustic-campaign/README.md), [hypothesis graph](../research/notes/sleep-acoustic-campaign/hypotheses.json), and [reference protocol](../research/notes/sleep-acoustic-campaign/protocols/confirmatory_association.json) concern a narrow temporal association between predefined physiology events and independently recorded acoustic onsets.

The campaign explicitly excludes source, adaptation, mechanism, attribution, and intent from its initial claim. It is a development reference rather than a registered confirmatory human study. Registered controls, calibration, timestamp anchoring, and analysis freezing remain dependencies for any future study.
