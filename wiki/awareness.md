## Questions for the continuation review

- Which subjective reports and objective tasks test awareness of the actual feature carrying information: flicker, pattern, symbol, or meaning?
- What evidence can distinguish near-chance performance from an imprecisely measured null result? How should uncertainty, equivalence bounds, response bias, and competing statistical models be handled?
- Can selecting “unaware” people or trials, changing the awareness task, or measuring awareness at a different time create an apparent dissociation?
- Which findings survive independent replication and controls for residual perception, expectancy, attention, stimulus leakage, and recording artifacts?
- How do the inferences change across visual masking, imperceptible modulation, closed eyes while awake, non-lucid sleep, and lucid REM?
- What would demonstrate discrimination, semantic access, learning, or later transfer beyond an evoked response or an EEG classifier’s performance?

These are questions to investigate, not findings established by this documentation pass.
