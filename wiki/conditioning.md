# Conditioning, repetition, and suggestion

[Home](../README.md) · [Hypnosis](hypnosis.md) · [Sleep](sleep.md) · [Research network](research-horizon.md)

The original **Research conditioning and hypnosis** conversation distinguishes learning a response from obeying an instruction. Its recovered answers cover fear learning, anticipatory nausea, conditioned immune responses, repetition, familiarity, memory confidence, and false recollection.

## Separate mechanisms

- Classical conditioning pairs a cue with an event or response. A cue's acquired emotional or bodily effect does not encode an arbitrary command.
- Operant learning depends on consequences linked to behavior. Repetition of a phrase alone does not establish that contingency.
- Repetition may increase familiarity or change confidence; repeated checking can reduce confidence in memory. Neither effect establishes a universal induction method.
- Suggestion can influence reports, experience, and selected tasks. Accurate recall and confidence must be measured separately.
- Awareness is paradigm-dependent. Evidence about one fear-learning task cannot settle every form of conditioning without awareness.

The human [conditioned immunosuppression experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12468450/) is a primary-source lead from the recovered work. A conditioned laboratory endpoint does not show that necessary treatment can be replaced, or that all effects of a medicine can be reproduced by a cue.

## Self-generated language and bodily cues

The [semantic and kinesthetic note](../research/notes/vindication-semantic-kinesthetic-association.md) asks whether self-generated language, speech, typing, posture, movement, or haptic cues strengthen associations beyond matched passive exposure. It is a hypothesis map. Wrong-feature controls, matched word counts, expectancy checks, and ordinary arousal are necessary to interpret any proposed interaction.

These are research questions about measured responses. They do not establish an explanation for a particular person's experiences or an actor's intent. A result explained equally well by intensity, expectancy, ordinary learning, or selective recall would narrow the proposed mechanism.

## Imagery and involuntary memory

The newly recovered [mental-focus review](../research/chats/supplement-4d344059-64d6-4a61-8749-6ffa673c3947.md) collects motor imagery, voluntary versus hypnotic experience, attention, and neural comparisons. Its reader truncation was resolved before publication. The [imagery and source-monitoring review](../research/chats/supplement-61ab65d6-91b2-414d-a69f-599e081abc03.md) discusses prior processing and later involuntary memories, while separating awareness of a stimulus from awareness of an intention.

The [specific missing-combination assessment](../research/chats/supplement-9371b8a4-f8fe-49e9-a800-993bbf738a44.md) reports that the reviewed work did not cleanly demonstrate the proposed combination of immersive imagery, unnoticed speech, and a later specified response. Combining related studies is a hypothesis-generating synthesis, not an independent replication of that chain.

## Semantic ambiguity and behavioral inference

The deeper search recovered [wordplay as an experimental hypothesis](../research/chats/deeper-6e1eb6b0-d045-4619-a75c-ae7c3911b2f4.md), [subliminal cueing limits](../research/chats/deeper-6ddd5ee5-8e07-443c-aa45-0e267e54a7f0.md), and [source concealment versus conditioning](../research/chats/deeper-bea38127-ce72-43f1-9803-253e56139eac.md). Ambiguous speech, learning an association, changing an evaluation, and identifying intent require separate evidence.

The recovered [kinesthetics overview](../research/chats/deeper-8f804713-457a-4482-b222-f9e23e0216be.md) supplied no original inline citations. Its gesture examples remain hypotheses, not diagnostic rules. An independently checked [meta-analysis of deception judgments](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327957pspr1003_2) found average unaided accuracy of 54% across 206 documents and 24,483 judges. That is strong reason to avoid inferring deception or a particular internal state from an isolated gesture.

The [sound-habituation discussion](../research/chats/deeper-3c8ec32e-4cab-4cd0-9d03-506d7af30574.md) separates reduced awakening from increased suggestibility. Reduced overt response cannot establish that a stimulus has become more effective at influencing beliefs or behavior.
