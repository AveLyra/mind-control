The collection separates documented programs, research objectives, observed impairment, and claims of successful control. A program's interest in a technique does not establish that it achieved its objective.

## Drug effects and hypnosis are separate endpoints

The recovered work examines antimuscarinics including scopolamine, LSD suggestibility experiments, drug-combination studies, modern memory research, and funding attribution. Memory impairment, delirium, sedation, altered perception, increased suggestibility on a scale, and predictable complex behavior are different outcomes.

The second conditioning chat corrects an earlier omission by identifying a historical human scopolamine/LSD combination study. That correction is retained in the source material. Combination research does not establish that more impairment produces more useful or reliable control. Modern openly funded memory studies do not, by themselves, establish covert continuation of a historical program.

Historical drug and funding claims remain source-specific. Chemical similarity or a shared receptor cannot demonstrate equivalent psychological effects, historical use, or present exposure. Personal symptom discussions, procurement, and medical testing logistics are excluded from the public collection.
